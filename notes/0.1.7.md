Kind: feature

- **Comments stand apart.** Each comment is a card with the author's mark and name, when it was written, and what they said below it, so one comment no longer runs into the next.
- **Review comments show their file.** A comment on a line of a change shows the file as a short chip, with the whole path on hover, and a press opens that comment on the tracker. A leading word such as "Important:" is picked out.
- **Bots stay out of the way.** A comment the tracker says came from a bot is one quiet line with its gist, opened with a press. When an account posts the same report again and again, only the newest shows, with "5 earlier like this" to see the rest.
- **Badges say their label once.** A picture that cannot be shown is its label, said once, instead of a stand-in mark followed by the same words again, and a badge sitting before its own label is left out.
- **Hidden notes stay hidden.** The hidden markers some tools put in their comments are no longer shown as text.
