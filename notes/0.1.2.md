Kind: feature

- **Screenshots shown in place.** Pictures in a description or a comment appear as thumbnails as you scroll to them, with their alt text kept. Press one to see it full size, and Escape closes it.
- **Pictures stay private.** Kehikot fetches them with your own sign-in, only from the item's own tracker. A picture from anywhere else stays a link, with its site named, and nothing is loaded from a stranger's server.
- **Approvals at a glance.** A change says "Approvals: 1 of 2" when its tracker has an approval rule.
