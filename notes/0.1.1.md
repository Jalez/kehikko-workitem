Kind: feature

- **The pipeline as one badge per stage.** A merge request's checks are grouped into validate, build, test and the rest, each a badge with an icon and the stage's worst state, and a count when something is not passing. Hover a badge to see its jobs, or press it to open them in place.
- **A clear pipeline line.** "Pipeline failed · 2 of 34 failed", with Open pipeline beside it, replaces the run-together "Checks faileddetails". A job waiting for a person shows as manual and does not make its stage look failed.
- **Reviewers always shown.** A change lists each reviewer with whether they approved, asked for changes or are still pending, and says "None" when nobody is asked.
- **Named Work Item** everywhere it is shown.
