Kind: feature

- **Read one issue, merge request or pull request in full, without loading the tracker's website.** Select an item on the canvas and Work item shows its title, state, people, labels, milestone, description, discussion, checks, branches and the items it links to, from what Kehikot already read with your own sign-in.
- **Instant, then fresh.** What was read before shows at once, and the item is read again in the background; Refresh in the header asks again, and Open on the tracker's site is one press away.
- **Works offline.** When the tracker cannot be reached, what was read stays on screen with a line saying when it was read.
- **Something to do when nothing is selected.** The space's recently updated items are listed, and a press shows one here and selects it for the rest of the canvas.
- **Safe to read.** Descriptions and comments are shown as Markdown with no raw HTML, pictures are links rather than loaded, and every link opens the way Kehikot opens addresses.

## For agents

- `shown_item` (read-only) says which issue or change the person is looking at in a project: its ref, title, state, people, labels, branches, failed checks, comment count, linked items and when it was read.
